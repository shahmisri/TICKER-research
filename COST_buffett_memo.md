# COST — Buffett-style business memo

**Verdict: Own the business**

Costco shows durable business quality in the SEC-reported operating history. Revenue grew from 226,954 in fiscal 2022 to 303,154 in fiscal 2026, while net income grew from 5,844 to 9,226. ROE was 28.3%, 25.1%, 31.2%, 27.8%, and 25.8%; the script's 15% ROE check passes. Owner earnings were positive in every reported year and grew from 3,501 to 9,382, despite a decline to 6,629 in fiscal 2024. The script's owner-earnings checks both pass.

This is a judgment about the business, not its stock price. No price data was used.

**Do I trust this verdict?** I trust it as a business-quality conclusion because its figures and checks come from SEC EDGAR facts and explicit calculations; it does not determine whether the shares are attractively priced.
