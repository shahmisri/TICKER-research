# Graham-style business memo

**Verdict: Passes screen.**

- Current ratio of 2.0 or more, latest year: pass
- Net income positive every year: pass
- Net income grew, first year to last: pass

Threshold kept: 2.0 for the current ratio; it is a conservative screen, not a universal rule.

I trust this only as a limited defensive screen; it does not assess valuation or the full business.
