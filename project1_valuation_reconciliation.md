# Project 1 — Microsoft Valuation Reconciliation

## 1. Valuation Methods

Two valuation methods were developed for Microsoft Corporation (MSFT).

**FCFF DCF:** Estimates enterprise value by discounting forecast free cash flow to the firm using WACC, then adjusts for non-operating cash and debt to obtain equity value.

**FCFE Pro-forma:** Estimates equity value directly by discounting free cash flow to equity using the cost of equity.

## 2. Base-Case Results

| Metric | FCFF DCF | FCFE Pro-forma |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated value per share | $197.60 | $116.94 |
| Discount rate | 10% | 10% |
| Terminal growth rate | 3% | 3% |
| Forecast period | FY2027–FY2031 | FY2027–FY2031 |

The FCFF-based valuation exceeds the FCFE-based valuation by $80.66 per share.

## 3. Differences in Model Assumptions

The FCFF DCF begins with FY2026 FCFF of $69,408.22 million and projects five years of cash-flow growth using rates of 15%, 13%, 11%, 9%, and 7%.

The FCFE pro-forma begins with FY2026 revenue of $331,839 million and forecasts revenue growth of 12%, 11%, 10%, 9%, and 8%.

The pro-forma explicitly incorporates operating expenses, depreciation, taxes, capital expenditures, inventory investment, and changes in other assets.

The pro-forma also assumes capital spending increases from $120,000 million in FY2027 to $140,000 million in FY2031, reflecting continued investment in AI and cloud infrastructure.

## 4. Interpretation

The difference between the two valuations is driven by different projected cash-flow amounts and modeling assumptions. The pro-forma's capital-intensive forecast is one potential contributor to its lower valuation.

The results should not be interpreted as interchangeable estimates produced from identical operating and financing assumptions.

## 5. Outstanding Reconciliation

Before selecting a final investment recommendation, the analysis should:

- Compare annual FCFF and FCFE forecasts.
- Reconcile financing flows and debt assumptions.
- Check the treatment of cash and non-operating assets.
- Compare terminal values and terminal-value dependence.
- Verify the consistency of discount rates and growth assumptions.
- Assess whether capital expenditures and cash-flow forecasts are supported by Microsoft's financial disclosures.

Until these checks are completed, the two valuations should be presented as separate model outputs rather than a fully reconciled valuation range.

### Interpretation

The updated FCFF DCF produces an estimated intrinsic value of $197.71 per diluted share, compared with $116.94 under the FCFE pro-forma model.

The valuation difference is approximately $80.77 per share.

The FCFF model forecasts higher cash flows throughout FY2027–FY2031 and generates a larger present value of terminal cash flows. The FCFF model also incorporates a separate adjustment for non-operating cash less debt.

The FCFE pro-forma model uses explicit operating forecasts and substantial projected capital expenditures for Microsoft's cloud and AI infrastructure.

These models use different cash-flow definitions and forecast assumptions. Their numerical differences should not be interpreted as a fully reconciled economic valuation.

### Investment Implications

Both model estimates remain below the September 1, 2026 reference share price of $501.02.

The results support continued consideration of a watch/defer investment recommendation, subject to final verification of historical inputs, forecast assumptions, and valuation methodology.
