### Ratio Definitions

**Gross margin** = Gross profit ÷ Revenue

**SG&A ÷ gross profit** = (Sales & marketing + General & administrative) ÷ Gross profit

**Inventory days** = Inventory ÷ Cost of revenue × 365

**Reported growth** = Current-year revenue ÷ Prior-year revenue − 1
**Depreciation ÷ PP&E** = Depreciation expense ÷ year-end net PP&E

Microsoft does not report a company-wide same-store growth metric comparable to an auto dealer such as ABG. For Microsoft, reported company revenue growth and business-specific growth measures such as Microsoft Cloud and Azure growth are more relevant operating indicators.

# Personalization

ABG's model emphasizes inventory and floor-plan financing because vehicle inventory is a major balance-sheet and financing item. Microsoft has no comparable floor-plan line. For Microsoft, I replace that emphasis with PP&E and capital spending because Microsoft's cloud and AI infrastructure requires substantial investment in datacenters, servers, CPUs, GPUs, and related infrastructure.

Microsoft's net PP&E increased substantially across the historical period, while additions to PP&E rose from $44.5 billion in FY2024 to $64.6 billion in FY2025 and $115.9 billion in FY2026. Because of this, my model cannot simply carry ABG's fixed capital-spending assumption forward.
